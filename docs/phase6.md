# PolyPulse Phase 6: Cross-Venue Intelligence & Arbitrage Scanner
# Architecture & Implementation Report

## 1. Executive Overview & Problem Statement

Cross-venue prediction market operations between Polymarket and Kalshi present unique quantitative and infrastructure challenges. While both venues offer binary contracts ($0.00 to $1.00 settlement), their microstructure, contract specifications, legal jurisdictions, settlement mechanisms, and outcome definitions differ significantly:

1. **Title Matching Hazard**: Market titles alone (e.g., *"Fed interest rate decision in March 2026"*) never establish contract equivalence. One exchange may define settlement based on the target upper bound published at 2:00 PM EST, while another uses effective Fed Funds rate benchmarks or press conference remarks.
2. **Outcome Orientation Disparity**: Contracts can be directly equivalent ($Poly_{\text{YES}} \equiv Kalshi_{\text{YES}}$) or inversely equivalent ($Poly_{\text{YES}} \equiv Kalshi_{\text{NO}}$). Failing to model inverse equivalence misses major market opportunities or causes fatal directional mispricings.
3. **Settlement & Expiration Cutoff Risk**: Polymarket settles via UMA Oracle or on-chain resolution using USDC on Polygon, while Kalshi is a CFTC-regulated Designated Contract Market (DCM) settling in USD. Differing resolution timestamps or fallback provisions introduce non-fungible basis risk.

Phase 6 delivers a high-reliability **Cross-Venue Intelligence and Arbitrage Scanner** grounded in an operator-reviewed contract registry, canonical price normalization, depth-aware VWAP calculations, versioned fee modeling, an 8-state opportunity machine, persistent ClickHouse analytics, and a real-time React terminal workspace.

## 2. Operator-Reviewed Contract Registry Architecture

### 2.1 Extended Contract Pair Model
Each tracked cross-venue pair stores verified metadata covering all required dimensions:

```sql
CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS contract_pairs (
  polymarket_asset_id TEXT NOT NULL,
  kalshi_ticker TEXT NOT NULL,
  canonical_event TEXT NOT NULL,
  canonical_outcome TEXT NOT NULL,
  event_description TEXT NOT NULL,
  outcome_equivalence TEXT NOT NULL,
  equivalence_mapping TEXT NOT NULL CHECK(equivalence_mapping IN ('direct', 'inverse')),
  resolution_source TEXT NOT NULL,
  resolution_criteria TEXT NOT NULL,
  polymarket_expiration TEXT NOT NULL,
  kalshi_expiration TEXT NOT NULL,
  expiration TEXT NOT NULL,
  settlement_details TEXT NOT NULL,
  verification_status TEXT NOT NULL CHECK(verification_status IN ('unverified', 'verified', 'rejected', 'incompatible', 'expired')),
  reviewer TEXT NOT NULL,
  verified_at TEXT,
  updated_at TEXT NOT NULL,
  PRIMARY KEY(polymarket_asset_id, kalshi_ticker)
);
```

### 2.2 Rejection Rules & Invariant Enforcement
1. **Zero Title-Only Matching Invariant**: Pairs generated by fuzzy string comparison or market title matching are strictly forbidden from entering execution.
2. **Explicit Settlement Condition Comparison**: Requires documented `resolution_source` and `resolution_criteria`. Any discrepancy in authoritative data sources causes `incompatible` status.
3. **Cutoff Disparity Filter**: If $|T_{\text{poly\_exp}} - T_{\text{kalshi\_exp}}| > 24\text{ hours}$ without explicit explanation in `settlement_details`, the pair cannot be marked as `verified`.
4. **Expiration Invalidation**: If $\min(T_{\text{poly\_exp}}, T_{\text{kalshi\_exp}}, T_{\text{exp}}) \le T_{\text{now}}$, the contract is classified as `expired` and immediately excluded from the scanner.
5. **Human Operator Verification Gate**: Only pairs with `verification_status == 'verified'`, non-empty `reviewer`, and valid `verified_at` enter the scanner.
6. **Operator Authentication**: All mutating endpoints require `X-Operator-Key` or `Authorization: Bearer <key>`.

## 3. Mathematical Model of Cross-Venue Arbitrage

Prediction markets trade binary outcome tokens where $P_{\text{YES}} + P_{\text{NO}} = 1.00$.

### 3.1 Direct Equivalence Mapping ($Poly_{\text{YES}} \equiv Kalshi_{\text{YES}}$)

#### Leg 1: Long Polymarket / Short Kalshi
- Buy Polymarket YES at best ask $P^{\text{poly}}_{\text{ask}}$
- Sell Kalshi YES at best bid $P^{\text{kalshi}}_{\text{bid}}$ (or buy Kalshi NO at $P^{\text{kalshi\_NO}}_{\text{ask}}$)
$$\text{Spread}_{\text{poly}\to\text{kalshi}} = P^{\text{kalshi}}_{\text{bid}} - P^{\text{poly}}_{\text{ask}}$$

#### Leg 2: Long Kalshi / Short Polymarket
- Buy Kalshi YES at best ask $P^{\text{kalshi}}_{\text{ask}}$
- Sell Polymarket YES at best bid $P^{\text{poly}}_{\text{bid}}$
$$\text{Spread}_{\text{kalshi}\to\text{poly}} = P^{\text{poly}}_{\text{bid}} - P^{\text{kalshi}}_{\text{ask}}$$

#### Executable Depth & Capacity
$$\text{Executable Qty} = \min(Q^{\text{poly}}, Q^{\text{kalshi}})$$
$$\text{Executable Notional} = \text{Executable Qty} \times P_{\text{entry}}$$

---

### 3.2 Inverse Equivalence Complementary Payoff Parity ($Poly_{\text{YES}} \equiv Kalshi_{\text{NO}}$)

When outcomes are inverted, holding Poly YES and Kalshi YES covers all possible states of the world (one must settle to \$1.00 and the other to \$0.00).

> [!WARNING]
> **Synthetic Payoff Parity vs. CTF Minting**:
> Holding a cross-venue synthetic position across two separate exchanges is NOT on-chain CTF minting or merging. In the Conditional Token Framework, minting refers strictly to depositing USDC collateral directly into the Gnosis CTF exchange contract on Polygon to receive token pairs. Cross-venue payoff parity is a synthetic position subject to inter-venue legal, settlement, and oracle basis risk.

#### Strategy 1: Synthetic Long Both (Complementary Payoff Parity)
- Buy Polymarket YES at $P^{\text{poly}}_{\text{ask}}$
- Buy Kalshi YES at $P^{\text{kalshi}}_{\text{ask}}$
- Combined cost: $\text{Cost} = P^{\text{poly}}_{\text{ask}} + P^{\text{kalshi}}_{\text{ask}}$
- Settlement payout is \$1.00 in all states of the world:
$$\text{Spread} = 1.00 - (P^{\text{poly}}_{\text{ask}} + P^{\text{kalshi}}_{\text{ask}})$$
- **Inventory Requirement**: Cash capital on both venues only (no short inventory required).

#### Strategy 2: Synthetic Short Both (Complementary Short Parity)
- Sell Polymarket YES at $P^{\text{poly}}_{\text{bid}}$
- Sell Kalshi YES at $P^{\text{kalshi}}_{\text{bid}}$
- Combined upfront revenue received: $\text{Revenue} = P^{\text{poly}}_{\text{bid}} + P^{\text{kalshi}}_{\text{bid}}$
- Maximum combined payout liability is \$1.00:
$$\text{Spread} = (P^{\text{poly}}_{\text{bid}} + P^{\text{kalshi}}_{\text{bid}}) - 1.00$$
- **Inventory Requirement**: Requires pre-existing token inventory on both venues or posting 100% collateral ($1.00/contract) to mint complete sets before selling; binary prediction markets do not support naked shorting.

#### Real-World Risk Factors
No cross-venue opportunity is "riskless" or "guaranteed":
1. **Legging Risk**: Market prices moving between executing leg 1 and leg 2.
2. **Partial Fills**: Unhedged directional exposure when one venue fills partially.
3. **Settlement / Oracle Divergence**: Polymarket settles via decentralized UMA dispute consensus; Kalshi settles via official government reports and CFTC determinations. Divergent resolutions can result in both positions losing or being delayed.
4. **Capital Lockup**: Full collateral remains locked until contract expiration with zero yield.

### 3.3 Net Spread & Regulatory Fee Haircut Engine

Gross spreads are adjusted for regulatory and venue taker fee schedules across both execution legs:
$$\text{Net Spread} = \text{Gross Spread} - \text{Fee}_{\text{buy\_venue}} - \text{Fee}_{\text{sell\_venue}} - \text{Slippage}_{\text{both\_legs}}$$

- **Kalshi Official CFTC Taker Fee (`v2026.2`)**:
  Calculated per transaction based on series multipliers and rounded up to the nearest whole cent:
  $$\text{fee\_cents} = \min\left(\lceil \text{multiplier} \times C \times P \times (1 - P) \times 100 \rceil, \text{max\_fee} \times 100 \times C\right)$$
  - Standard series (`FED`, `INTRATE`, `CPI`, `REC`, `POL`): multiplier **$0.07$ (7%)**, capped at $\$0.02$ per contract.
  - Index series (`INDEX`, `SPX`, `NASDAQ`): multiplier **$0.035$ (3.5%)**, capped at $\$0.0175$ per contract.
  - Rounding rule: `ceil_whole_cents` at order level; minimum fee: $\$0.01$ per executed order.
- **Polymarket Official Taker Fee Formula & API Normalization**:
  Polymarket taker fees are rounded to 5 decimal places. The market's fee-curve exponent $e$ is applied to the price component:
  $$\text{fee} = \text{round}\left(C \times r \times [P(1 - P)]^e, 5\right)$$
  where $\text{feeRate}$ is a **dimensionless formula coefficient** (NOT a simple flat percentage of contract notional).
  - **Resolution Precedence**:
    1. **Token-Specific Official API Configuration**: Gamma (`/markets?clob_token_ids=<id>`) resolves the condition ID. CLOB `GET /clob-markets/{condition_id}` supplies `fd.r`, `fd.e`, and `fd.to`, preserved as coefficient, exponent, and taker-only flag. A complete Gamma `feeSchedule` can be used if CLOB market information is unavailable. `/fee-rate?token_id=<id>` returns `base_fee` in **basis points** only; this is metadata, not a complete fee curve, and cannot authorize an executable classification by itself.
    2. **Explicit Rate / Fee-Disabled Flag**: Honors explicit `poly_fee_rate`, `poly_taker_bps`, or `fees_enabled = False` ($\text{feeRate} = 0.0$).
    3. **Official Category Coefficients (`POLYMARKET_CATEGORY_FEES`)**:
       - **Finance** (`finance`, `finance_prices_fees`): $\text{feeRate} = 0.04$
       - **Politics** (`politics`, `politics_fees`): $\text{feeRate} = 0.04$
       - **Economics** (`economics`, `economics_fees`): $\text{feeRate} = 0.05$
       - **Geopolitics** (`geopolitics`, fee-free): $\text{feeRate} = 0.00$
       - **Other Official Categories**: Crypto $0.07$, Sports $0.05$, Tech $0.04$, Culture $0.05$, Weather $0.05$, General/Other $0.05$.
    4. **Missing or Invalid Fee Configuration**: If the fee configuration is missing or invalid, the fee is classified as `fee_status = "unknown"`, `net_spread = None`, and the scanner **never** claims a positive executable net spread (`status = "candidate"`, `is_arbitrage = False`).
  - **CLOB `fd` edge cases**: An explicit `fd: null` is fee-free only when the CLOB response itself, or the token-matched Gamma market, explicitly reports `feesEnabled=false`. A successful CLOB response with no `fd`, a malformed `fd`, or unavailable fee information remains unknown. An unreadable CLOB response does not inherit a category fee merely because the market title or fee type is available.
  - **Depth execution fees**: `vwap_by_size` now includes fee status, both leg fees, total fees, net spread, estimated net spread, and executable status. Polymarket's curve is evaluated at each filled level's actual price and quantity, with five-decimal rounding per fill before aggregation. Kalshi sums each level's raw fee and applies the cent ceiling once for the order. The headline top-quote result is capped at the first level's quantity; it does not price deeper liquidity at the top price. Depth executable flags also respect the scanner's stale-market gate. Decimal arithmetic is used for fee amounts and rounding (`ROUND_HALF_UP` for five-decimal Polymarket estimates; `ROUND_CEILING` for Kalshi cents). Source coefficients are retained without eight-decimal truncation. The exact Polymarket tie convention has not been verified against live fills.
- **Estimated Slippage Across Both Execution Legs**:
  Cross-venue execution requires entering two distinct market legs. The slippage buffer ($S_{\text{bps}} = 10\text{ bps}$) is applied per leg across each leg's notional value:
  $$\text{Slippage}_{\text{buy}} = P_{\text{buy}} \times \frac{S_{\text{bps}}}{10{,}000} \times Q, \quad \text{Slippage}_{\text{sell}} = P_{\text{sell}} \times \frac{S_{\text{bps}}}{10{,}000} \times Q$$
  $$\text{Total Slippage Cost} = \text{Slippage}_{\text{buy}} + \text{Slippage}_{\text{sell}}$$
- **Reference Scenarios (Direct & Inverse Execution)**:
  *(Note: These are deterministic reference scenarios verifying mathematical correctness, not live trading results.)*
  - **Reference Example 1 (Direct: Long Poly Finance / Short Kalshi FED)**:
    - Quantity: $C = 100$, Poly Finance Ask: $0.40$ ($\text{feeRate} = 0.04$), Kalshi Bid: $0.46$ (`FED` series, $0.07$ multiplier).
    - Gross Spread: $\$0.46 - \$0.40 = \$0.06/\text{contract} \implies \mathbf{\$6.00}$.
    - Polymarket Fee: $\text{round}(100 \times 0.04 \times 0.40 \times 0.60, 5) = \mathbf{\$0.96}$.
    - Kalshi Fee: $\lceil 100 \times 0.07 \times 0.46 \times 0.54 \times 100 \rceil / 100 = \lceil 173.88 \rceil / 100 = \mathbf{\$1.74}$.
    - Total Known Fees: $\$0.96 + \$1.74 = \$2.70$; Net After Known Fees: $\$3.30$.
    - Two-Leg Slippage (10 bps/leg): $\$0.040 + \$0.046 = \mathbf{\$0.086}$.
    - Expected Net (after fees & slippage): $\$6.00 - \$0.96 - \$1.74 - \$0.086 = \mathbf{\$3.214}$ ($\$0.03214/\text{contract}$).
  - **Reference Example 2 (Direct: Long Kalshi FED / Short Poly Politics)**:
    - Quantity: $C = 100$, Kalshi Ask: $0.42$ (`FED` series, $0.07$ multiplier), Poly Politics Bid: $0.48$ ($\text{feeRate} = 0.04$).
    - Gross Spread: $\$0.48 - \$0.42 = \$0.06/\text{contract} \implies \mathbf{\$6.00}$.
    - Polymarket Fee: $\text{round}(100 \times 0.04 \times 0.48 \times 0.52, 5) = \mathbf{\$0.9984}$.
    - Kalshi Fee: $\lceil 100 \times 0.07 \times 0.42 \times 0.58 \times 100 \rceil / 100 = \lceil 170.52 \rceil / 100 = \mathbf{\$1.71}$.
    - Total Known Fees: $\$1.71 + \$0.9984 = \$2.7084$; Net After Known Fees: $\$3.2916$.
    - Two-Leg Slippage (10 bps/leg): $\$0.042 + \$0.048 = \mathbf{\$0.090}$.
    - Expected Net (after fees & slippage): $\$6.00 - \$1.71 - \$0.9984 - \$0.090 = \mathbf{\$3.2016}$ ($\$0.032016/\text{contract}$).
  - **Inverse Example 3 (Complementary Long Both `mint_both_long`)**:
    - Quantity: $C = 100$, Poly Politics Ask: $0.45$ ($\text{feeRate} = 0.04$), Kalshi FED Ask: $0.48$ ($0.07$ multiplier).
    - Gross Spread: $100 \times (1.00 - [0.45 + 0.48]) = \mathbf{\$7.00}$.
    - Polymarket Fee: $100 \times 0.04 \times 0.45 \times 0.55 = \mathbf{\$0.99}$; Kalshi Fee: $\lceil 100 \times 0.07 \times 0.48 \times 0.52 \times 100 \rceil / 100 = \mathbf{\$1.75}$.
    - Two-Leg Slippage: $\$0.045 + \$0.048 = \mathbf{\$0.093}$; Expected Net: $\$7.00 - \$2.74 - \$0.093 = \mathbf{\$4.167}$.
- **No Guaranteed Profit**: PolyPulse enforces `is_guaranteed_profit = False` across all opportunities. Payoff parity and spreads are subject to execution delays, partial fills, capital lockup, and resolution oracle divergence.

## 4. Opportunity State Machine & Synchronization Invariants

The scanner classifies every evaluated pair into one of 8 deterministic states:
1. `candidate`: Top-of-book gross spread $> 0$, but net spread after fees is $\le 0$.
2. `executable`: Verified pair, fresh quotes, net spread $> 0$, and non-zero executable depth.
3. `insufficient_liquidity`: Top-of-book prices imply an opportunity, but zero executable depth is available.
4. `stale`: Either venue quote age exceeds $T_{\text{max}} = 10.0\text{s}$, or venue timestamp divergence $|\Delta T| > 5.0\text{s}$.
5. `invalid_book`: Either venue exhibits crossed books ($P_{\text{ask}} \le P_{\text{bid}}$), zero prices, or negative spreads.
6. `unverified_contract`: Contract pair has not been operator-reviewed or verified.
7. `expired`: Current time exceeds contract expiration cutoff.
8. `closed`: Market status is halted, settled, or closed on either exchange.

## 6. Endpoints, WebSocket & Notification Engine

### 6.1 FastAPI Endpoints (`/api/v1/cross-venue/`)
- `GET /api/v1/cross-venue/pairs`: List contract pairs with status filter.
- `GET /api/v1/cross-venue/pairs/{pair_id}`: Retrieve pair details.
- `PUT /api/v1/cross-venue/pairs/{pair_id}/review`: Create/update pair review (Operator auth).
- `GET /api/v1/cross-venue/spreads`: Live scanned spreads across all verified pairs.
- `GET /api/v1/cross-venue/opportunities`: Filtered positive net spread opportunities.
- `GET /api/v1/cross-venue/opportunities/{opportunity_id}`: Single opportunity detail.
- `GET /api/v1/cross-venue/fees`: Active fee schedules and fee calculation breakdown.
- `GET /api/v1/cross-venue/analytics`: Historical ClickHouse analytics and duration stats.
- `GET /api/v1/cross-venue/notifications`: Recent notification audit log.

### 6.2 Dedicated Real-Time WebSocket (`/ws/cross-venue`)
- Streams periodic frames containing active opportunity states, top-of-book quotes, and executable depth.
- Operates concurrently on an async task loop without blocking backend ingestion.

### 6.3 Notification Engine (`backend/notifications.py`)
- Emits events for `candidate_opportunity`, `executable_quote`, and `invalidated_opportunity`.
- Enforces per-pair cooldown timers (default 30 seconds) to prevent alert storms.

## 9. Strict Engineering Boundaries Audit

1. **Zero Trade Execution**: PolyPulse Phase 6 is purely an intelligence, monitoring, and analytics engine. No live orders are submitted to either exchange.
2. **Credential Redaction**: No API keys, private keys, or passwords are baked into source files or sent via unauthenticated channels.
3. **Zero Title-Only Matching**: Pairs must be explicitly reviewed with resolution source and criteria; string heuristic matching is strictly forbidden.
4. **Durability Guarantee**: All quotes, reviews, and opportunities transit through the SQLite WAL outbox with SHA-256 tokens before ClickHouse insertion.
